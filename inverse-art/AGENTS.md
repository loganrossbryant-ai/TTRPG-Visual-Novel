## Scope and source of truth
The Inverse is a school-appropriate science-fiction TTRPG: international-school students in China descend to the ocean floor, find a hatch in Station Zero, and enter an inverse reality with suspended seas, distorted gravity, parallel lives, secret civilizations and alien infrastructure. Inspirations: The Leviathan Chronicles (immortals, hidden agendas, secret civilizations, deep-ocean mystery), Event Horizon (industrial cosmic dread without graphic horror). Weird, wondrous, dark. No high fantasy, magic academies or generic medieval ornamentation. Students remain protagonists, adults facilitate or obstruct. Predominantly Chinese and Korean students with some Western students, natural live-action faces, ordinary age-appropriate expedition clothing. No gore or sexualization. Proposed final revelation, not yet locked: both Earths are fragments of a deliberately separated larger reality, and something wants reunification. Neither world is original. Do not overwrite existing visual novel or game code. Work on this branch or a new child branch, in inverse-art/ only.

## Art direction and production
Photoreal live-action film stills: real skin pores, hair, believable anatomy, concrete, steel, ceramic, glass, water, practical costume construction, physically plausible light and camera optics. Surreal colors and impossible physics remain vivid; don't flatten everything into gray realism. Different palettes: Hanging City cyan/amber/violet; Memory Forest emerald/pale aqua; Founders ivory/gold; Blackdoor charcoal/red; Resistance warm amber/rust; Fracture Zone vermilion/magenta; Dead Sun black/gold; salt basins bright white; frozen regions violet/blue. Prioritize variety in silhouettes, compositions and subject matter. Each generation request concerns exactly ONE subject, one image; never feed whole batch as prompt or generate montage. No labels in art except intentional readable clue handouts; no board crops or upscaled thumbnails passed as original assets. Aim at native high resolution (prior examples often 1536x1024 or 1648x928), landscape for classroom projection, portrait for NPCs as appropriate. Use available image generation capability; check availability/limits first. Do not use paid external services without approval. If unavailable, deliver exact prompts and mark blocked, not imaginary files.

## Factions
Founders preserve separation: porcelain-white tech, gold, symmetry. Blackdoor seeks gate control: matte-black angular gear, red optics. Resistance (Seinshun, youth allies) seeks truth: repurposed machinery, amber light. Independents (Harlequinn/Tulley) seek travel freedom: salvaged expedition gear.
