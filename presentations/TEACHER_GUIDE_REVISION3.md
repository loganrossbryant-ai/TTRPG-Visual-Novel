## Play and navigate

Open the downloaded PPTX in desktop PowerPoint and enter **Slide Show**. Background videos start automatically and repeat; the teacher advances manually with the right arrow, remote or small next arrow. Normal/editing view and PDF show still posters. The MP4 preview demonstrates the actual animated scenes with their projected text, for review on a computer or phone without PowerPoint.

Each session opening has a bottom-right **MAP** box. The map has **RETURN TO SESSION** and **ATLAS** buttons. Detailed atlas pages have direct session-number buttons, full-map/zoom links and interior links. The appendix is at the bottom of the file and hidden from ordinary linear Slide Show advancement; hyperlinks still open it. Session 6 links to Orpheus, not the future colony archive. K-17 outpost is optional investigation/remote planning; the bible does not mandate a visit.

- Boarding clouds move inside a strict sky matte; the ship and people remain still.
- Practical lights slowly glow and dim. Black-hole accretion light flows and pulses without decorative orbiting dots. Comms and archive terminals emit blue/green light.
- Every NPC portrait and clue has a moving context or physical prop. Original standalone portraits remain the identity references; restored group scenes follow those faces. Named characters keep their original identities.
- Physical maintenance ticket, anomaly instrument, unsynchronized moving clocks, biometric trace, identity terminal, power console, class photograph and final logbook replace abstract placeholder cards. Incoming blue transmission reveals on the monitor; its warning remains a separate beat.
- Crew argument, drone incident, restricted door, adult reaction, evidence pull, adult confrontation, aftermath, final debate and optional K-17 outpost have explicit animated scenes.
- All six possible encounters use the latest supplied enemy artwork. The tactics page shows weakness, behaviour, special actions and objective pressure; its linked dossier page retains type, threat, role and stage cues. All dossier text is native/editable. Bible encounter numbers override the maintenance dossier’s minor-drone profile.
- Real supplied map layouts replace word-box schematics. Maps have restored illustrative detail, subtle light/water/vegetation/snow motion where relevant, readable native keys and enlarged detail views. Fine restored texture is illustration, not recovered survey information. Raster artwork has finite detail; native labels remain sharp when enlarged.
- The approved derelict hallway with small dark rats is reused byte for byte.
