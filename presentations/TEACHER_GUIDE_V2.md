The original atlas was excluded because it exposed future locations in Session 1. The full supplied enemy dossier remains a separate reference.

## Art and motion
The approved derelict v4 loop is reused exactly. Revision 2 replaces cosmetic motion with independently twinkling source stars; moving cloud color/alpha layers over fixed terrain; actual leaf and tree silhouettes anchored to stems; falling rain and wet-floor ripples; pane-confined rivulets; nozzle-attached flowing exhaust; emitted ballistic sparks; and moving steam. Fixed architecture and native faces/evidence are protected. No fog in vacuum, no rats on Pioneer, no orbiting black-hole ornaments. NPC and clue scenes inherit their current location. Reactor and anomaly scenes are more active; school and final reflection are restrained. Original uploads remain unchanged.
